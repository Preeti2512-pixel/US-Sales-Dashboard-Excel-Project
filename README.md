# 📊 US Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard (MS Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built on the **US Superstore dataset** (2014–2017), analyzing **$2.30M in total sales**, **$286K+ in net profit**, and **37,873 units sold** across **49 US states**. This project uncovers product-level revenue drivers, category profitability trends, seasonal demand spikes, and top profit-generating customers.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Top Sub-Category Drivers:** **Phones ($330K)** and **Chairs ($328K)** are the highest revenue-generating sub-categories, followed by **Storage ($224K)** and **Tables ($207K)**.
* **Category Profitability Gap:** **Technology ($145K+ profit)** and **Office Supplies ($122K+ profit)** demonstrated strong, consistent profit growth from 2014 to 2017, whereas **Furniture ($18K+ profit)** lagged significantly in profitability despite high sales volume.
* **Seasonal Sales Peaks:** Monthly revenue surges sharply in the final quarter—peaking in **November ($352K)**, **December ($325K)**, and **September ($308K)**.
* **Regional & Customer Concentration:** **California** leads all states in sales volume, while active customer counts grew steadily each year through 2017. Among the top 5 profit-generating customers, **Tamara Chand (27%)** and **Raymond Buch (21%)** contribute nearly half of the group's profit.

## 🛠️ Excel Skills & Techniques Demonstrated
* **Data Modeling & Pivot Tables:** Aggregated 9,994 transactional records into dynamic summary tables for sub-category sales, yearly profit trends, and customer counts.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Connected **Year** (2014–2017) and **Category** (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology) slicers across all six visuals for instant cross-filtering.
* **Custom Number Formatting:** Applied conditional formatting codes (`[>0]0,"K";0` and `M`) to streamline axis scales, funnel labels, and map legends.
* **Executive Dashboard Design:** Combined Funnel, Multi-Series Line, Filled Map, Horizontal Bar, Pie, and Area charts into a clean, full-screen layout.

2. Open the file in Microsoft Excel (2016 or later recommended).
3. Click any **Year** or **Category** button in the top slicer bar to explore filtered performance metrics.
